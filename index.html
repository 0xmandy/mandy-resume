01 2026.02 · 个人项目Personal Project 行情 · 自动化Markets · Automation

黄金白银价格监控 — Telegram 全自动推送 Gold & Silver Price Monitor — fully automated Telegram alerts

背景BACKGROUND
贵金属价格波动剧烈，人工盯盘既耗时又容易漏掉关键时点，需要一个 7×24 无人值守的价格监控。 Precious-metal prices swing hard; watching quotes manually is time-consuming and misses key moments. I needed 24/7 unattended price monitoring.
挑战CHALLENGE
免费行情源的稳定性与反爬限制；告警规则要过滤噪音、避免重复轰炸；脚本要在远程服务器上长期稳定自跑。 Free market-data sources are unstable and rate-limited; alert rules had to filter noise and avoid duplicate spam; the script had to run unattended on a remote server for the long term.
方案SOLUTION
Python 脚本定时轮询行情源，按价格阈值与波动幅度触发告警，经 Telegram Bot 推送到手机；部署在境外 VPS 上由 cron 定时驱动，token 等敏感配置全部走环境变量。 A Python script polls market-data sources on schedule, triggers alerts on price thresholds and swing magnitude, and pushes to my phone via a Telegram bot; deployed on an overseas VPS driven by cron, with all tokens kept in environment variables.
价值IMPACT
把「主动盯盘」变成「被动接收」：关键波动第一时间到手机，零人工值守。 Turned "actively watching quotes" into "passively receiving alerts": key moves reach my phone instantly, with zero human babysitting.

A股基本面研究系统 — 财报提取 RAG + 多 Agent 管线 A-Share Fundamental Research System — financial-report RAG + multi-agent pipeline

背景BACKGROUND
A股年报、招股书动辄数百页，人工找口径、对齐同业极其耗时；而直接「跟 AI 聊」得到的数字不可信——投研场景里每个数字都必须可溯源到原始文件。 A-share annual reports and prospectuses run hundreds of pages; manually locating figures and aligning peer comparisons is painfully slow. And numbers from "just chatting with AI" can't be trusted — in investment research, every figure must trace back to the source document.
挑战CHALLENGE
第一手信源纪律（招股书 / 年报 / 公告为据）；同口径的同业对比；数字可回溯；研究过程要沉淀成可复用的流水线，而不是一次性对话。 First-hand-source discipline (prospectuses / annual reports / filings only); peer comparisons on consistent definitions; traceable numbers; and a research process that compounds into a reusable pipeline instead of one-off conversations.
方案SOLUTION
「文件系统即真理」的研究仓库 + 固定 10 问的买方级事实底稿框架；LangGraph 多 Agent 管线分工——确定性工具负责取数与量化计算，LLM Agent 负责撰写，条件边把不合格的稿件打回重写；Streamlit dashboard 公网部署，Telegram 推送兜底。 A "filesystem as source of truth" research repo plus a fixed 10-question buy-side fact-sheet framework; a LangGraph multi-agent pipeline with clear separation — deterministic tools handle data retrieval and quantitative computation, LLM agents handle drafting, and conditional edges send substandard drafts back for rewrite; a Streamlit dashboard deployed publicly, with Telegram push as backup.
价值IMPACT
落地两份完整个股事实底稿（迈威生物 688062、嘉友国际 603871），每个结论附第一手出处；管线复用后，新增覆盖标的只需替换输入。 Shipped two complete single-stock fact sheets (Mabwell 688062, Jiayou International 603871), every conclusion backed by a first-hand citation; with the pipeline in place, covering a new company only requires swapping the inputs.

03 2026.06 – 至今Present · 个人项目Personal Project 行业研究 · 具身智能Industry Research · Embodied AI

机器人产业知识图谱 — 已发布 8 篇 HTML 研究合集 Robotics Industry Knowledge Atlas — 8 published HTML research pieces

背景BACKGROUND
具身智能是资本最热的赛道之一，但公开讨论充斥二手转述和情绪叙事。我要用基本面研究的方法论，做一套从术语、发展史到文献奠基人、月度跟踪的产业链知识库。 Embodied AI is one of the hottest sectors in venture capital, yet public discussion is full of second-hand retellings and hype. I set out to apply fundamental-research methodology to build an industry knowledge base spanning terminology, category histories, foundational literature, and monthly tracking.
挑战CHALLENGE
跨机械、感知、AI 多学科的术语门槛；历史叙事容易被后见之明扭曲，必须回到当年的一手材料；每个商业化判断都要能被反例证伪，而不是讲故事。 Terminology cuts across mechanics, perception, and AI; historical narratives get distorted by hindsight, so claims must trace back to primary materials from the time; and every commercialization judgment has to survive falsification against counterexamples — not just tell a good story.
方案SOLUTION
三条纪律贯穿全站：第一手信源为据、结构化框架、不确定处诚实标注。用「术语拟人图鉴」降低阅读门槛，用清洁机器人等已跑完商业化周期的品类做参照系，提炼出品类商业化的两条件判断框架，并用割草机器人等品类回测证伪。AI 负责检索与核验加速，结论由人工把关。 Three disciplines run through the whole site: first-hand sources only, structured frameworks, and honest flagging of uncertainty. A "personified terminology field guide" lowers the reading barrier; categories that have completed their commercialization cycle (e.g. cleaning robots) serve as reference systems, distilled into a two-condition framework for category commercialization — back-tested for falsification against categories like robotic lawn mowers. AI accelerates retrieval and verification; conclusions stay human-vetted.
价值IMPACT
已发布 8 篇 HTML 研究作品，形成可复用的品类商业化分析框架；同一套「信源纪律 + AI 加速」的工作流，可以迁移到任何硬科技赛道的尽调与行业研究。 Eight HTML research pieces published, plus a reusable framework for analyzing category commercialization; the same "source discipline + AI acceleration" workflow transfers to due diligence and industry research in any hard-tech sector.

04 2026.07 · 个人项目Personal Project 金融 · 基本面研究Finance · Fundamental Research

两份买方级深度底稿 — 万华化学 · 嘉友国际，可点开读全文 Two Buy-Side Deep-Dive Reports — Wanhua Chemical & Jiayou International, full text online

背景BACKGROUND
光说搭了个研究系统没有说服力，真正能被翻开、被追问的是系统跑出来的完整报告。我挑了两家生意模式截然不同的 A股公司来验证方法论：万华化学（重资产化工制造）和嘉友国际（跨境物流服务）。 "We built a research system" convinces no one on its own; what holds up to scrutiny is a full report the system produced. I picked two A-share companies with completely different business models to test the methodology: Wanhua Chemical (asset-heavy chemical manufacturing) and Jiayou International (cross-border logistics).
挑战CHALLENGE
两个行业的护城河逻辑毫不相通：化工拼的是成本曲线与投产节奏，物流拼的是通道控制与节点卡位。每个判断都得顶住反证——竞争者能否复制、政策生变会怎样、数字对不对得上年报口径。 The two industries share no moat logic: chemicals compete on cost curves and capacity-ramp timing, logistics on corridor control and node positioning. Every judgment had to survive counter-evidence — can a rival replicate it, what if policy shifts, do the numbers reconcile to the annual report.
方案SOLUTION
同一套「固定 10 问 + 第一手信源纪律 + 反证清单」的框架分别落到两家公司。万华拆解了每一次投产究竟把哪项成本压下去的成本演化链条，并与 BASF、科思创、亨斯迈、陶氏做同口径对照；嘉友以铜带多走廊时代的节点价值重画竞争地图，配一张可交互货流地图，把公路、铁路、陆港的角色一次讲清。 The same framework — a fixed 10 questions, first-hand-source discipline, and a falsification checklist — was applied to both. For Wanhua I traced the cost-evolution chain of exactly which cost each capacity expansion drove down, benchmarked on consistent definitions against BASF, Covestro, Huntsman, and Dow; for Jiayou I redrew the competitive map around node value in the copper-belt multi-corridor era, with an interactive cargo-flow map laying out the roles of road, rail, and dry ports at a glance.
价值IMPACT
两份完整买方底稿，每个结论都挂第一手出处（年报、招股书、公告、竞争对手 10-K）。同一框架不挑行业、可复用，是能沉淀下来的研究能力。 Two complete buy-side fact sheets, every conclusion tied to a first-hand source (annual reports, prospectuses, filings, competitors' 10-Ks). The same framework is industry-agnostic and reusable — a research capability that compounds.
