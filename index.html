机器人产业知识图谱 — 已发布 8 篇 HTML 研究合集 Robotics Industry Knowledge Atlas — 8 published HTML research pieces

背景BACKGROUND
具身智能是资本最热的赛道之一，但公开讨论充斥二手转述和情绪叙事。我要用基本面研究的方法论，做一套从术语、发展史到文献奠基人、月度跟踪的产业链知识库。 Embodied AI is one of the hottest sectors in venture capital, yet public discussion is full of second-hand retellings and hype. I set out to apply fundamental-research methodology to build an industry knowledge base spanning terminology, category histories, foundational literature, and monthly tracking.
挑战CHALLENGE
跨机械、感知、AI 多学科的术语门槛；历史叙事容易被后见之明扭曲，必须回到当年的一手材料；每个商业化判断都要能被反例证伪，而不是讲故事。 Terminology cuts across mechanics, perception, and AI; historical narratives get distorted by hindsight, so claims must trace back to primary materials from the time; and every commercialization judgment has to survive falsification against counterexamples — not just tell a good story.
方案SOLUTION
三条纪律贯穿全站：第一手信源为据、结构化框架、不确定处诚实标注。用「术语拟人图鉴」降低阅读门槛，用清洁机器人等已跑完商业化周期的品类做参照系，提炼出品类商业化的两条件判断框架，并用割草机器人等品类回测证伪。AI 负责检索与核验加速，结论由人工把关。 Three disciplines run through the whole site: first-hand sources only, structured frameworks, and honest flagging of uncertainty. A "personified terminology field guide" lowers the reading barrier; categories that have completed their commercialization cycle (e.g. cleaning robots) serve as reference systems, distilled into a two-condition framework for category commercialization — back-tested for falsification against categories like robotic lawn mowers. AI accelerates retrieval and verification; conclusions stay human-vetted.
价值IMPACT
已发布 8 篇 HTML 研究作品，形成可复用的品类商业化分析框架；同一套「信源纪律 + AI 加速」的工作流，可以迁移到任何硬科技赛道的尽调与行业研究。 Eight HTML research pieces published, plus a reusable framework for analyzing category commercialization; the same "source discipline + AI acceleration" workflow transfers to due diligence and industry research in any hard-tech sector.
